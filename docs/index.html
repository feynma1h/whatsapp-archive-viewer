Export a chat from WhatsApp

  1. On your iPhone, open the chat and tap its name at the top.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Export Chat.
  3. Choose Attach Media to include photos, videos and voice notes — or Without Media for a small text-only file.
  4. Save the .zip it produces: AirDrop it to your computer, or use Save to Files and copy it over later.
  5. Open it here — Choose export .zip files, or just drag it in.

Freeing up your phone: once an export opens correctly here, you can clear that chat's media on the phone (WhatsApp → Settings → Storage & Data → Manage Storage). Keep the zips somewhere safe — from then on they're your only copy.

Anything shown as an "omitted" placeholder was left out by WhatsApp at export time and is not in the zip. Android's export format isn't supported yet.
